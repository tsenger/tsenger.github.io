Privacy Policy - Sealva

Sealva verifies Visible Digital Seals (VDS) directly on your device. The app works without an account, advertising or tracking. This policy explains which data is nevertheless processed, why, and what rights you have.

2. Summary

  • Scanned seals are evaluated on your device. Names or other personal details contained in a seal never leave your device.
  • The app downloads verification certificates and reference data from my server. To do so, the server has to process your IP address. It is not stored permanently, except in shortened form as described in section 4.3.
  • The app does not create any device or installation identifier. Usage statistics are anonymous.
  • There is no advertising, no profiling and no disclosure of your data to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Using the App on Your Device

Camera

Sealva uses the camera only to scan barcodes. Camera images are neither stored nor transmitted.

Barcode recognition on Android (Google ML Kit)

On Android, the app recognizes barcodes with Google ML Kit, provided through Google Play services. Recognition takes place on your device; according to Google, camera images are not sent to Google. However, ML Kit may send technical usage and performance data to Google (for example device and app information or how long recognition took) and may download recognition models and updates from Google servers. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. A transfer to the USA cannot be ruled out; Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in reliable barcode recognition (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More information: ML Kit terms and Google Privacy Policy.

On iOS, the app uses Apple's barcode recognition, which works entirely on the device.

Scan history

Scanned seals are stored only locally on your device. You can switch off saving in the settings and delete single entries or the entire history at any time. I have no access to this data.

Local storage

On your device, the app stores only what it needs to work: settings, the scan history, and downloaded certificates and reference data (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG).

4. Connections to My Server

For some functions, the app connects to my server backend.tsenger.de. It runs on a virtual server of Contabo GmbH, Aschauer Straße 32a, 81549 Munich, Germany, in a data center in Germany. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Contabo. All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

4.1 When the app contacts the server

  • Verification certificates: on first start, then automatically at intervals and whenever you request an update. Without them, the app cannot verify signatures.
  • Municipality directory and postal codes: to display location information from seals in a readable way.
  • Version check: on every start, the app asks whether a minimum version is set and, if necessary, suggests an update.
  • Official documents: if the app does not yet know the document profile or signing certificate of an official seal, it requests it from my server. Only the profile number or the certificate fingerprint (SHA-1) and the source of the issuing authority stated in the seal are sent, never personal details from the document. If my server does not know the entry either, it loads it from the issuing authority itself. The authority only sees my server, not your device.

4.2 Data transmitted

With every connection, your device technically transmits:

  • your IP address,
  • date and time,
  • the requested address,
  • the app identifier (user agent), e.g. Sealva/2.9.0 (Android; shared 1.7.0): app version, platform and version of the shared app core.

4.3 How I use this data

  • Delivering the data: the IP address is needed to send the response to your device.
  • Protection against abuse: to prevent anyone from using my server to query third-party servers en masse, loading official documents is limited per IP address. For this, the server keeps the IP address in memory for at most one hour; it is not stored.
  • Log of loaded documents: when my server loads an entry from an issuing authority because of your request, I log the loaded address, the result, the user agent and your shortened IP address (IPv4 without the last block, IPv6 only the first 48 bits). This lets me detect abuse. These entries are deleted automatically after 90 days. The stored profile or certificate only keeps the address it was loaded from and the app version that requested it.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: I count how often each function is used per day and with which app version. These counters contain neither IP addresses nor any identifier of your device, so they cannot be traced back to individual persons. They help me decide how long older app versions need to be supported.
  • Server logs: the web server in front of the backend logs requests including the IP address for troubleshooting and to defend against attacks. The logs are rotated weekly and deleted automatically: access logs after at most five weeks, error logs after at most eleven weeks.

Legal basis: my legitimate interest in providing the app's functions, operating the server securely and reliably, and developing the app further (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Links and Other Apps

If you tap a link in the app, for example to a website or an address, it opens in your browser or another app. The privacy policy of the respective provider applies there.

6. App Stores

If you download Sealva from Google Play or the Apple App Store, these providers process data under their own responsibility, such as your account and the download. I have no influence on this; their privacy policies apply.

7. Contacting Me by Email

If you write to me, I process your email address, your name (if provided) and the content of your message in order to answer your request. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in answering inquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I keep your messages for as long as is necessary to deal with your request.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to

  • access the data I hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR).

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): where I process data on the basis of my legitimate interest, you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. An informal email to sealva@tsenger.de is sufficient.

Please note: since I do not recognize your device, I usually cannot attribute the data described in section 4 to a person (Art. 11 GDPR). The anonymous statistics contain no personal data at all.

Right to lodge a complaint: you may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the authority responsible for me: Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen, Kavalleriestraße 2-4, 40213 Düsseldorf, Germany, www.ldi.nrw.de.

9. Other Information

Provision of data: you are not obliged to provide any data. Without a server connection, however, the app cannot load current certificates or verify unknown official documents.

Automated decisions: there is no automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

Changes: I update this privacy policy when the app or the legal situation changes. The version published here applies.
